Help, scoring and privacy

Choose a question and press Go to question to practise it directly, or repeat it. Back returns to the previous question you visited. Next question follows your answer's branch, or the first example route if you have not answered. Alternative questions are also available in the list. Moving freely keeps your points without awarding them twice; this practice is not saved as a completed dialogue. Restart dialogue to begin a full run.

Guided shows Czech and English. Practice shows Czech. Exam hides the question text until you request a hint. Exam is a practice mode, not an official exam assessment.

Each dialogue starts at 10 points. An accepted answer earns its stated reward once per question. A hint costs 2 points once per question. After three unsuccessful attempts, you move on and lose 1 point once per question. Points stay at zero or above. Switching modes keeps your attempts; scores after switching during a run are saved as “Mixed modes”.

Different answers can be correct: you can ask questions, refuse, or choose an alternative. Feedback checks your response in the conversation. It does not assess pronunciation. If an answer is not understood, try saying it another way.

Speech recognition works in supported browsers such as Chrome and may need an internet connection. Allow microphone access only when you want to speak. If nothing is heard, check your microphone and site permissions. Czech voice availability depends on your device.

Your browser’s speech service may process audio remotely. This app does not store audio or answers. Only completed-run summaries are saved on this device, if browser storage is available. Clearing site data removes them.
